Savings, Wealth & Retirement Simulator

Build savings, diversify investments, grow wealth and plan retirement income while accounting for inflation.
About this simulator - Purpose & How to Use
This is a planning and scenario tool, not a prediction. Savings builds a fund from money you commit to save. Wealth & Investment manages investment capital. Cash in Hand is available capital not currently allocated to Savings or Wealth. Retirement uses whatever financial capital is in hand at retirement to test income and capital preservation.
You can use any planner independently. Collectively, the app follows one accounting rule: one asset, one balance, one calculation. Retirement never duplicates capital already held in Savings, Wealth or Cash in Hand.

1. What's the committed monthly savings fund?
The savings commitment is the money you put aside. The annual increase changes your contribution amount; it is not an investment return.
2. How do you plan to allocate the savings fund?
Select an investment or add your own. State the allocation percentage and planning return for each.
Guide: allocation and planning return
Allocation tells the simulator where each month's committed savings goes. Planning return tells it the annual return assumption for that investment. Each holding compounds separately.
RM1,000/month with 50% in Bank Deposit and 50% in MGS/GII means RM500/month goes to each. Each portion then compounds using its own planning return.

Guide: what Retirement does
Retirement is an income and capital-preservation strategy applied to the financial assets already in hand. The Savings and Wealth holdings keep their own planning returns. Cash in Hand earns 0% until it is allocated elsewhere. Retirement distributions reduce those same underlying balances. No duplicate retirement asset is created.
