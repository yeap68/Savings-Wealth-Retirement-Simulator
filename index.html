Savings, Wealth & Retirement Simulator

Plan your retirement from the savings and investments you already have, and the investments you will continue building. The simulator brings them into one retirement portfolio, projects each investment to your retirement age, and estimates the retirement drawdown and capital preservation those resources may support.
You do not need to complete everything at once. Start with what you know and update your portfolio whenever things change. Your assumptions remain visible and editable. Your plan is stored locally in this browser; no bank or brokerage connection is required.

How it works

1
Tell us about you

Set your current age, target retirement age and how long you want the plan to cover.

2
Build your portfolio

Add EPF, deposits, stocks, ETFs, bonds, gold and other retirement investments.

3
Project each investment

Each investment follows its own contribution period and planning return up to retirement.

4
See your retirement outlook

Explore projected capital, monthly drawdown, purchasing power and capital longevity.

5
Keep it up to date

Return anytime and update only what changed. Your saved portfolio remains intact.
