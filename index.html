Savings, Wealth & Retirement Simulator

Plan your retirement from the savings and investments you already have, and the investments you will continue building. The simulator brings them into one retirement portfolio, projects each investment to your retirement age, and estimates the retirement drawdown and capital preservation those resources may support.
You do not need to complete everything at once. Start with what you know and update your portfolio whenever things change. Your assumptions remain visible and editable. Your plan is stored locally in this browser; no bank or brokerage connection is required.

How do you contribute to EPF?

For a Malaysian member below age 60, the illustrative employer rate defaults to 13% at salary up to RM5,000 and 12% above; employee 11%. Rates are editable planning percentages, not exact statutory payroll deductions. KWSP uses wage bands/rounding for wages up to RM20,000; rules differ by age, citizenship and member category. Review your payslip. Bonus is modelled as EPF-contributable in the final month of each year.
Official reference: KWSP contribution schedule
No employer contribution or government incentive is assumed.

Your Fixed Deposit

Your deposit and quoted rate apply to the first term. At each maturity, principal and interest are rolled over. Future renewals use your separate planning rate; they are not guaranteed at today's quoted rate. A final incomplete term accrues interest pro rata on an effective annual basis.
Illustrative FD compounding over longer periods

Illustrations use your future renewal assumption at a constant rate; these are NOT historical Maybank, CIMB, Public Bank or Hong Leong Bank returns. Verified comparable bank-specific renewal series for all requested horizons are not available in this build.

Your Gold Holding

Live Malaysian gold price: unavailable in this static build. Enter a dated price you can actually sell at; physical purity, spreads and bank buyback quotes differ. Do not use a bank's selling-to-you price to value an existing holding.
