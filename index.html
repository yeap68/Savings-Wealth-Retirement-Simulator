3. How these assets affect your retirement plan
Investment return
Each Savings and Wealth holding continues using its own planning return. Higher assumed returns can support more income or more ending capital, but planning returns are assumptions, not guarantees.
Cash and liquidity
Cash in Hand is an option inside Savings. It remains available and liquid and earns 0% until you reallocate it to another Savings or investment asset, so a larger unallocated cash balance can reduce portfolio growth.
Asset allocation
The mix of assets determines the combination of planning returns supporting retirement. Retirement does not replace those assets with one new blended retirement account.
Retirement distributions
Monthly retirement income is withdrawn from the same underlying assets. The current default withdraws proportionally across the portfolio, so future returns are earned on the remaining balances.

5. Choose your retirement objective
Compare both the income and the consequence. A higher monthly distribution is not automatically better: it may leave less protection against inflation or lower-than-planned returns.
Compare the three retirement strategies
Maximum Distribution prioritises the highest starting income while targeting preservation of the original nominal RM capital.

Preserve Real Capital targets a higher future nominal balance so that the ending capital retains approximately the purchasing power of the starting capital after inflation.

Balanced deliberately starts at 2.4% of retirement capital per year. It is not the mathematical midpoint between the other two strategies; it is a conservative withdrawal rule intended to leave a larger capital buffer invested.

How to compare: look at Starting income, Total retirement income, Projected ending capital, Ending capital in today's RM, Nominal Capital status and Real Capital status together.
How are these amounts calculated?
Each underlying Savings and Wealth asset keeps its own planning return. The simulator converts each annual return to an effective monthly rate, applies that return to the opening monthly balance, and then deducts the retirement distribution proportionally across the underlying assets.

If retirement income escalation is enabled, the monthly distribution increases once each year by the selected escalation rate. Inflation is used separately to measure purchasing power and, for Preserve Real Capital, to calculate the nominal ending-capital target required to preserve starting purchasing power.

Planning returns are assumptions, not guarantees. Actual investment returns, inflation and sequence of returns can differ materially from the projection.

Guide: what Retirement does
Retirement is an income and capital-preservation strategy applied to the financial assets already in hand. The Savings and Wealth holdings keep their own planning returns. Cash in Hand inside Savings earns 0% until it is allocated elsewhere. Retirement distributions reduce those same underlying balances. No duplicate retirement asset is created.
